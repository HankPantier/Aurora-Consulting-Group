# About Aurora Consulting Group LLC

## Identity
Aurora Consulting Group LLC was founded in 2011 in Visalia, California, by Sandra L. Koch, who serves as CEO and Founder. The firm's tagline — "Your team for tax and accounting and a whole lot more" — reflects its mission to empower entrepreneurs by building enduring relationships through strategic tax planning, meticulous accounting, insightful advisory, and supportive coaching.

Aurora describes its team as advisory-focused, operating as an extension of each client's business rather than a detached, transactional vendor. The firm's tone is warm, approachable, and client-centered — professional, proactive, empowering, and trustworthy — while deliberately avoiding stiff or automated communication.

The team includes Sandra L. Koch (CEO-Founder), Connor Brooks (Client Controller), Andrea Dunigan (Client CFO), Alannah Dunkle (Client Service Manager), Annette Shuler (Business Support & Operations Coordinator), Cedar Gonzalez (Executive Assistant to the CEO), and team members Haydee Lamberty-Rodriguez and Maureen Lykins.

## Positioning & Differentiation
Aurora positions itself as the outsourced financial team that goes beyond numbers — an embedded advisory partner that handles accounting and tax while actively driving client growth and decisions.

Key differentiators include:
- A "Team of 3" approach — a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO — delivering the expertise of three professionals for less than the cost of one full-time hire.
- Enrolled Agents with IRS representation authority.
- Fixed monthly pricing with no annual contracts.
- Customized technology stacks and secure digital workflows tailored to each client.
- A fully remote, culture-driven team.

Aurora's ideal clients are entrepreneurs and business owners who value integrity in their financial data and relationships, hold high standards for delivering value to their own customers, and seek proactive problem-solving from a responsive financial team. This includes professionals and consultants (legal, medical, financial, engineering/architecture, real estate, healthcare/social services), technology companies from startups to established SaaS and e-commerce businesses, and nonprofit organizations.

Clients typically need support with HR, corporate compliance, insurance, and multi-state/multi-entity operations, and benefit from frequent advisory meetings. The firm's clients are primarily aged 35–55 and find Aurora through referrals, Google search, networking, and its website.

Geographically, the majority of Aurora's clients are based in California, including Visalia (where the firm was founded), Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, Kern County, and Central California stretching from Chico down to San Diego. Aurora is a BBB Accredited Business and a listed Gusto Partner.

## Industries Served

### Real Estate Investors
Aurora provides tax strategy, entity structuring, and accounting for real estate investors managing rental portfolios, fix-and-flip projects, and long-term wealth building across multiple properties and entities. Common pain points include depreciation schedules and cost segregation tracking across multiple properties, entity structure confusion (LLC, S-corp, partnership) as portfolios grow, multi-state income allocation and filing requirements, passive activity loss rules and material participation tracking, 1031 exchange documentation and deadline compliance, and inconsistent cash flow visibility across properties.

### Real Estate Brokers
Aurora offers outsourced accounting, tax planning, and advisory services for real estate brokers and agents navigating commission-based income, self-employment tax, and the financial complexity of running an independent practice. Pain points include inconsistent cash flow tied to commission cycles, self-employment tax planning and quarterly estimated payments, tracking business expenses (marketing, MLS fees, vehicle, home office) across engagements, entity structure decisions as income scales, lack of proactive tax strategy, and multi-state licensing and income allocation when closing deals across state lines.

### Contractors
Aurora delivers bookkeeping, job costing, and tax planning for contractors and trades businesses managing project-based revenue, subcontractors, payroll, and the financial demands of a growing field operation. Pain points include job costing and project profitability tracking across multiple active jobs, payroll and subcontractor 1099 compliance, cash flow gaps between billing and payment cycles, equipment depreciation and asset management, entity structure decisions as the business scales, and sales tax and licensing compliance across jurisdictions.

### Business-to-Business Service Providers
Aurora supports accounting, tax strategy, and advisory for B2B service companies — including consulting firms, staffing agencies, marketing agencies, and professional service businesses — focused on profitability, cash flow, and scalable growth. Pain points include tracking billable vs. overhead expenses across client engagements, inconsistent cash flow tied to client payment cycles and net terms, entity structure confusion as revenue and headcount grow, lack of proactive tax strategy, HR/payroll/contractor compliance as teams scale, and multi-state income allocation for businesses with clients or staff in multiple states.

### Multi-State / Multi-Entity Businesses
Aurora provides specialized accounting, compliance, and advisory support for businesses operating across multiple states or structured through multiple legal entities, built for owners who need a fully embedded financial team that understands the full picture. Pain points include state registration, nexus, and compliance obligations across jurisdictions; intercompany transactions and consolidated reporting; inconsistent financial data when entities are managed separately; entity structure optimization for tax efficiency and liability protection; payroll tax compliance for remote or multi-state employees; and the lack of a unified advisory partner who understands the full picture across all entities.
