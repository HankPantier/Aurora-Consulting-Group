## Proactive tax strategy instead of once-a-year filing

Most tax preparers show up once a year, hand you a return, and disappear until next April. That's filing. It isn't strategy.

At Aurora Consulting Group, planning starts months before year-end and keeps going after the deadline passes. Your Team of 3, a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, stays on your file year-round, watching for opportunities as your business changes rather than reacting to what already happened.

Quarterly estimates get built on real numbers, not guesses. Entity elections get reviewed before they cost you money, not after the return is filed.

Founded in Visalia, California, we built this firm for entrepreneurs and professionals who wanted an accountant asking questions in June, not just in March. If your current preparer only calls when something's due, this is the shift you've been looking for.

## Strategic planning for entrepreneurs and professionals

Tax planning that works for a SaaS founder doesn't work for a physician, and what works for a physician doesn't work for a nonprofit executive director. We build strategy around your entity type, income pattern, and growth stage.

### Professionals and Consultants
icon: Briefcase

Attorneys, physicians, financial advisors, engineers, architects, and real estate professionals often operate as S-corporations or partnerships with income that swings by quarter. We review reasonable compensation, retirement contribution limits, and deduction timing so self-employment tax doesn't eat into a strong year.

### Technology Companies
icon: Zap

From early-stage SaaS startups to established e-commerce businesses, tech companies face decisions around R&D credits, equity compensation, and multi-state sales tax exposure as they scale. Planning happens before the next funding round or product launch, not after the books close.

### Nonprofit Organizations
icon: Users

Nonprofits carry their own compliance calendar, including unrelated business income tax, Form 990 filings, and donor restriction tracking. We help boards and executive directors stay ahead of filing requirements while keeping tax-exempt status intact.

## Navigating multi-state and multi-entity tax complexity

A lot of our clients don't fit into one state or one entity anymore. A real estate investor picks up a property in Kern County and another out of state. A consulting firm hires a remote employee in Texas. A multi-entity operation in Fresno County needs consolidated reporting across three LLCs and an S-corp.

Each of those moves creates a nexus question, and nexus questions create registration and filing obligations most business owners never see coming until a notice arrives. We determine where you owe, register you correctly, and build consolidated reporting that shows the full picture across entities instead of three disconnected returns.

Clients across Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, Kern County, and Central California come to us specifically because multi-state and multi-entity structures are where generalist preparers tend to miss things. It's the kind of work that requires someone watching intercompany transactions all year, not just at filing time.

## IRS representation from Enrolled Agents you can trust

An IRS notice in the mailbox is stressful even when you've done nothing wrong. Our Enrolled Agents hold federal authority to represent you directly before the IRS on audits, collections, and appeals, so you're not the one on the phone trying to decode agency language alone.

Enrolled Agent status is granted by the IRS itself, not a state license, which means the same credential applies whether your issue starts in Visalia or spans multiple states. Here's what that representation covers:

- Audit representation and response to IRS correspondence
- Negotiating installment agreements and offers in compromise
- Resolving back taxes and unfiled returns
- Appeals and penalty abatement requests

You get someone with legal standing to speak for you, not just someone who prepared the return.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Tax Planning & Strategy

**Q: What is the difference between tax planning and tax preparation?**
A: Tax preparation reports what already happened on a return filed once a year. Tax planning happens throughout the year, before decisions are locked in, so your Team of 3 can adjust entity structure, deductions, and estimates while there's still time to act.

**Q: Can Aurora Consulting Group represent me if I receive an IRS notice?**
A: Yes. Our Enrolled Agents hold IRS-granted authority to represent clients directly on audits, collections, and appeals. You get someone with legal standing to communicate with the IRS on your behalf instead of handling the notice alone.

**Q: Do you handle tax planning for businesses operating in more than one state?**
A: Yes. We determine nexus, handle state registration, and build consolidated reporting for clients with operations across California and beyond, including multi-entity structures with intercompany transactions between LLCs and S-corps.

**Q: How is your tax planning and strategy service priced?**
A: Fixed monthly pricing with no annual contract. You know the cost before work starts, and you aren't locked into a long-term agreement if your needs change or the fit isn't right.
