## A team of three built around you

Every client at Aurora Consulting Group gets three specialists, not one generalist stretched thin across fifty accounts. Your Client Service Manager coordinates day-to-day requests, keeps your books current, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks between meetings. Your Client Controller reviews the financials, catches discrepancies before they become problems, and manages the accounting detail that keeps your numbers audit-ready. Your Client CFO sits above the numbers and translates them into decisions: pricing, hiring, entity structure, and the timing of a big purchase.

Most businesses can't afford to hire a controller and a CFO separately, let alone both. A single senior accounting hire in California often clears six figures once payroll taxes and benefits are added in. The Team of 3 model delivers that same depth, plus a dedicated point of contact, for a fixed monthly fee that runs less than one full-time salary. Three people already understand your business before you ever have to explain it twice.

## Enrolled Agents you can trust with the IRS

When the IRS sends a notice, most bookkeepers can only tell you to call a CPA and start over. Our team includes Enrolled Agents, federally licensed to represent clients directly before the IRS. That means the person who already knows your books can also stand in front of the IRS's own agents on your behalf, without a handoff to a stranger.

- Direct representation during audits, appeals, and collections, no outside referral required
- Faster response to notices because your EA already knows your financial history
- Authority recognized in all 50 states, useful for clients operating across state lines
- One team from tax planning through resolution, so you never re-explain your situation

## Advisors who become part of your business

Aurora Consulting Group is built as a fully remote, culture-driven firm on purpose. Skipping the overhead of a traditional office means your fee goes toward advisory time, not rent. It also means your team is available on your schedule, not confined to one branch location in Visalia.

Clients describe the relationship as tight-knit rather than transactional. Meetings happen often enough that your CFO already knows what's coming before you bring it up. Questions get answered the same week, sometimes the same day. HR questions, corporate compliance filings, insurance renewals, a new state registration: these get routed to the right person on your team instead of bouncing between departments. You end up with a financial team that behaves like it works down the hall, even when it works three counties or three states away.

## Specialized experience across real estate, contracting, and multi-entity businesses

Our advisory work centers on five recurring niches, alongside the professional, technology, and nonprofit sectors we prioritize for firms like architecture practices, SaaS companies, and social service organizations. Each niche below comes with its own compliance quirks, and our team has built specific workflows around them.

### Real Estate Investors
icon: Building2

Depreciation schedules and cost segregation tracking across multiple properties, plus entity structure decisions between LLCs and S-corps that keep liability and tax exposure separated deal by deal.

### Real Estate Brokers
icon: Home

Commission-cycle cash flow smoothing and quarterly estimated tax planning built around self-employment income that swings month to month.

### Contractors
icon: Hammer

Job costing and profitability tracking across active projects, subcontractor 1099 compliance, and payroll structured around seasonal crew changes.

### B2B Service Providers
icon: Briefcase

Billable-versus-overhead tracking across client engagements and cash flow planning when payment terms stretch 30, 60, or 90 days out.

### Multi-State and Multi-Entity Businesses
icon: Globe

State registration, nexus determination, and consolidated reporting across jurisdictions, plus intercompany transaction tracking between related entities.

## Rooted in Visalia, serving Central California and beyond

Aurora Consulting Group started in Visalia, California, and most of our clients are still based across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties. That local footing shapes how the team works: fast response times, familiarity with California filing requirements, and a real understanding of what growing a business in Central California actually looks like.

Being remote from the start let that same team take on clients with operations in other states without missing a step. Multi-state and multi-entity businesses get the same Team of 3 structure, the same fixed monthly pricing, and the same Enrolled Agent representation, wherever the entities are registered.
