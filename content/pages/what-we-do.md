## A full-service accounting team without the full-time hire

Running a growing business means the books can't wait, but hiring a full accounting department isn't realistic for most owners. Aurora Consulting Group built its outsourced accounting service around a simple idea: give clients three specialists for less than the cost of one full-time hire.

Every client gets a [Team of 3](/meet-the-team/your-team): a Client Service Manager who handles day-to-day communication and deadlines, a Client Controller who manages the accounting detail, and a Client CFO who brings strategic financial guidance to the table. That structure means you're never waiting on one overworked bookkeeper or guessing who to call with a question.

Founded in Visalia, California, Aurora works with entrepreneurs, professionals, technology companies, and nonprofits across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties who want a financial team that acts like part of the business, not an outside vendor. The Team of 3 model replaces the guesswork of hiring with a structured, accountable partnership.

## What our outsourced accounting service includes

Full-service bookkeeping and back-office accounting cover the transactional work that keeps a business financially sound, without pulling you away from the work only you can do.

- Day-to-day transaction management and bank and credit card reconciliations
- Monthly financial statements delivered on a consistent schedule
- Accounts payable and receivable tracking
- Payroll processing coordination and support
- Custom reporting built around the metrics that matter to your business
- A recurring cadence of check-ins so nothing sits unresolved

When the books close on time every month, you get real financial statements to make decisions from, not a shoebox of receipts sorted once a year. That's the standard the Client Controller and Client CFO hold every engagement to.

## Technology and workflows tailored to your business

Every business runs on different software, and forcing a client into one fixed system rarely works. Aurora builds a technology stack around what you already use, then layers in secure digital workflows for document sharing, approvals, and reporting.

As a Gusto Partner, Aurora connects payroll data directly into your accounting workflow, cutting down on manual entry and the errors that come with it. Bank feeds, receipt capture, and reporting dashboards get configured to match how you already operate, not the other way around. The result is a system built around your business, updated as it grows, and secured with the same standards Aurora applies to its own client data.

## HR, compliance, and insurance guidance as you grow

Growing past a handful of employees brings problems that have nothing to do with debits and credits. Multi-entity clients in particular run into HR questions, compliance deadlines, and insurance gaps that a transactional bookkeeper won't flag. Aurora's Client CFOs raise these issues in [advisory sessions](/what-we-do/advisory-coaching) before they become expensive.

### HR support
icon: Users

From onboarding paperwork to classification questions between employees and 1099 contractors, the team helps you stay compliant as headcount grows.

### Corporate compliance
icon: FileCheck

Annual filings, registered agent requirements, and entity maintenance across states get tracked so deadlines don't slip.

### Insurance guidance
icon: ShieldCheck

Coverage gaps show up fast in growing or multi-entity businesses. Aurora flags where a policy review is overdue.

## Built for multi-state and multi-entity complexity

Businesses operating in more than one state, or running multiple entities under one ownership group, face a different level of complexity: state registration requirements, nexus questions, and consolidated reporting that a single-state bookkeeper isn't built to handle.

Aurora's Client Controllers and Client CFOs work directly with [multi-state and multi-entity businesses](/industries/multi-state-multi-entity-businesses) across California, including clients based in Visalia, Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, and Kern County who have expanded operations into other states. That means tracking where you owe tax, reconciling intercompany transactions between related entities, and producing consolidated reporting that reflects the whole business, not just one piece of it.

This is the kind of work that benefits from frequent advisory meetings rather than a once-a-year phone call. Aurora structures its engagement around that reality, checking in regularly enough to catch a registration deadline or a nexus threshold before it becomes a compliance problem.

## Simple, predictable pricing with no long-term contract

Outsourced accounting pricing at Aurora is fixed and monthly. You know the cost before the engagement starts, and it doesn't change because of a slow month or a busy one.

There's no annual contract locking you in. If the fit isn't right, either side can end the engagement without penalty. That structure exists because Aurora would rather earn the relationship every month than rely on a signature from a year ago.

Compare that to hourly billing that punishes you for asking questions, or a bundled contract that locks you into services you don't need yet. Fixed monthly pricing keeps budgeting straightforward, and it keeps the Team of 3 accountable to deliver value, not just renew a contract.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Outsourced Accounting Solutions | Aurora Consulting Group LLC

**Q: What is outsourced accounting?**
A: Outsourced accounting means hiring an outside team, rather than in-house staff, to handle bookkeeping, reconciliations, and financial reporting. Aurora pairs each client with a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO for a fraction of one full-time salary.

**Q: How much does outsourced accounting cost?**
A: Aurora charges a fixed monthly fee with no annual contract, so pricing stays predictable regardless of transaction volume in a given month. The exact rate depends on entity count, transaction volume, and the level of advisory support your business needs.

**Q: Can Aurora handle multi-state or multi-entity businesses?**
A: Yes. Aurora's Client Controllers and Client CFOs manage state registration tracking, nexus questions, intercompany transactions, and consolidated reporting for clients operating across California and beyond, with frequent advisory check-ins built into the engagement.

**Q: Does outsourced accounting include payroll and HR support?**
A: Aurora is a Gusto Partner and coordinates payroll processing as part of the outsourced accounting service, plus guidance on HR compliance, employee classification, and insurance coverage gaps as your team grows.
