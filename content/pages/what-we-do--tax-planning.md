## Proactive tax planning that goes beyond filing season

Most tax preparers show up once a year, run the numbers, and send an invoice. That works fine for a hobby, but it leaves real money on the table for entrepreneurs building something bigger. Aurora Consulting Group treats tax planning as a year-round conversation, not a spring deadline.

We meet with clients throughout the year to model quarterly estimates, entity elections, and timing decisions before they get locked into a return. A contractor deciding whether to buy equipment in December or January. A consultant weighing an S-corp election against staying a sole proprietor. A tech founder tracking R&D expenses as they happen instead of reconstructing them in April. Each decision compounds, and catching it early is the difference between a strategy and a surprise.

Based in Visalia, California, our team works with business owners across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties who want a tax approach that keeps pace with how their business actually runs, not just how the calendar says it should be filed.

## Personalized strategy from your Team of 3

Tax strategy sharpens when more than one perspective is in the room. Every Aurora client is supported by a Client Service Manager, a Client Controller, and a Client CFO, three professionals who bring distinct expertise to the same strategy session instead of routing you through a single overworked preparer.

Your Client Service Manager keeps communication tight and deadlines visible. Your Client Controller makes sure the underlying books are clean enough to plan from, because tax strategy built on messy data is just guesswork. Your Client CFO connects the tax picture to your broader business goals: growth financing, owner compensation, multi-entity structuring, retirement contributions.

Clients get the combined judgment of three specialists for less than the cost of one in-house hire. That structure is why the same team filing your return in April is often the one flagging a planning opportunity in September.

## IRS representation from Enrolled Agents you can trust

A letter from the IRS rarely arrives at a convenient time, and most bookkeepers can't do much once it does. Aurora's tax team includes Enrolled Agents, federally licensed to represent clients before the IRS on any tax matter, in any state, without restriction. That authority matters when a notice, audit, or collections case lands on your desk.

Enrolled Agent status is granted directly by the IRS after passing a rigorous three-part exam covering individual and business tax law, or through prior IRS service. It carries the same representation authority held by attorneys and CPAs, meaning we speak for you directly instead of handing you a script to read to an examiner.

Here's where that authority gets used most often:

- Responding to IRS notices and information requests before deadlines pass
- Representing clients through federal audits, from initial inquiry to resolution
- Negotiating payment plans and offers in compromise on back taxes
- Correcting prior-year filings and resolving multi-year discrepancies

## Tax strategy for multi-state and multi-entity businesses

Operating across county or state lines multiplies the number of ways a return can go wrong. A real estate investor with properties in three counties. A B2B service firm with contractors in two states. A SaaS company with remote employees creating nexus in jurisdictions the founder has never visited. Each entity and each state carries its own registration, filing, and compliance calendar.

Aurora tracks state registration requirements, nexus thresholds, and intercompany transactions across the businesses we serve throughout Central California and beyond. When a client runs an LLC that owns a property management company billing a separate S-corp, we build the consolidated reporting structure that keeps those entities tax-efficient and audit-defensible, instead of three disconnected books that don't talk to each other.

This is where the Client CFO role earns its keep: mapping entity structure to tax outcome, not just closing the books at year-end.

## Specialized tax guidance for professionals, tech companies, and nonprofits

Tax planning changes shape depending on the business behind it. Three groups make up a large share of the entrepreneurs we work with, each carrying its own set of recurring tax questions.

### Professionals and Consultants
icon: Briefcase

Attorneys, physicians, engineers, and financial consultants often start as sole proprietors and outgrow that structure fast. We evaluate S-corp elections, retirement plan options, and reasonable-compensation calculations so self-employment tax doesn't quietly erode a strong year.

### Technology Companies
icon: Zap

From early-stage SaaS startups to established e-commerce businesses, tech companies face R&D credit eligibility, multi-state sales tax nexus, and equity compensation questions a generic preparer often misses. We track qualifying research expenses as they happen, not at filing time.

### Nonprofit Organizations
icon: HeartPulse

Unrelated business income, Form 990 accuracy, and donor restriction tracking carry real compliance risk. We keep nonprofit clients current on filing requirements while helping boards understand what the numbers mean for programmatic decisions.

## Fixed monthly pricing with no surprises

Tax planning shouldn't come with a bill that changes based on how many questions you asked this month. Aurora clients pay a fixed monthly fee that covers ongoing planning conversations, not just the return itself, and that fee doesn't lock anyone into a multi-year contract.

If your business changes, whether you add an entity, hire your first employee, or expand into a new state, your plan adjusts and so does the pricing conversation, openly and in advance. No retainer surprises, no by-the-hour clock running during a planning call. Clients tell us this is the difference between calling with a question and avoiding the phone because they're worried about the invoice.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Tax Planning & Preparation Services | Aurora Consulting Group

**Q: What is the difference between tax planning and tax preparation?**
A: Tax preparation is filing an accurate return once a year. Tax planning is the year-round strategy work, entity elections, timing decisions, and quarterly estimates, that determines what that return will actually say. Aurora does both, built around ongoing planning conversations.

**Q: Can Aurora Consulting Group represent me if I get an IRS notice?**
A: Yes. Aurora's tax team includes Enrolled Agents, federally licensed to represent taxpayers before the IRS on any matter in any state. That includes notices, audits, collections cases, and negotiating payment plans on back taxes.

**Q: Do you work with businesses operating in multiple states?**
A: Yes. Aurora tracks state registration requirements, nexus thresholds, and intercompany transactions for clients with multiple entities and multi-state operations, building consolidated reporting structures instead of disconnected, single-entity bookkeeping.

**Q: How is tax planning priced?**
A: Aurora charges a fixed monthly fee that covers ongoing planning conversations, not just the return itself. There is no annual contract, and pricing is discussed openly whenever your business, entities, or filing needs change.
