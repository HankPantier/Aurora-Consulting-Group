## Your dedicated team of three

Most firms hand you one contact and hope for the best. Aurora Consulting Group builds a Team of Three around every client: a Client Service Manager, a Client Controller, and a Client CFO. Each role covers a distinct layer of your financial operation, from day-to-day questions to long-range strategy, so nothing falls through the cracks between bookkeeping and the boardroom.

For less than the cost of one full-time accounting hire, you get three specialists working in concert. That's the model professionals, technology companies, and nonprofits across Visalia, Tulare County, and the wider Central Valley rely on when they've outgrown a single bookkeeper but aren't ready to build a finance department.

### Client Service Manager
Your first point of contact and the person who keeps communication moving. They coordinate deadlines, answer day-to-day questions, and make sure requests don't sit in an inbox.

### Client Controller
Owns the accuracy of your books: reconciliations, financial statements, and the details that make your numbers reliable enough to act on.

### Client CFO
Brings the strategic view, translating your financials into decisions about pricing, hiring, entity structure, and growth.

## A relationship built on advisory, not just entries

Compliance work tells you what happened. Advisory work helps you decide what happens next. Aurora Consulting Group builds every client relationship around the second half of that sentence.

Instead of a single annual meeting before tax season, you'll sit down with your Client CFO on a recurring schedule, reviewing margins, cash flow, and upcoming decisions while there's still time to act on them. We ask the questions a business owner is often too close to ask themselves: should this hire wait a quarter, is this the year to elect S-corp status, does this contract change your estimated tax payments.

That rhythm is what separates an embedded advisory partner from a firm that only shows up when a form is due. Entrepreneurs across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties use these conversations to plan the next twelve months, not just close out the last one.

## Support beyond the numbers

Growing a business means more than clean books. Payroll questions, contractor classifications, business insurance renewals, and state filings tend to land on a business owner's desk right alongside the numbers, and most of them can't wait for a slow response.

Aurora Consulting Group's Team of Three helps coordinate the pieces around your accounting, not just the accounting itself:

- HR guidance, including payroll setup and compliance support through our partnership with Gusto
- Corporate compliance tracking, from registered agent renewals to annual state filings
- Insurance coordination, flagging gaps before a claim or audit exposes them
- 1099 and contractor classification support for teams that mix employees and subcontractors
- IRS representation from Enrolled Agents when a notice or audit shows up in the mail

None of this replaces a dedicated HR or legal team once you're large enough to need one. It does mean fewer surprises while you're growing into that size.

## Built for multi-state and multi-entity complexity

California businesses that grow past one county rarely stay simple. A second entity, a second state registration, or a client base that spans Visalia and Sacramento each add a layer of compliance most in-house bookkeepers weren't built to track.

Aurora Consulting Group works with multi-state and multi-entity businesses across Central California, handling nexus questions, intercompany transactions, and consolidated reporting so ownership sees one clear financial picture instead of five disconnected sets of books. Your Client Controller tracks the mechanics; your Client CFO connects them to the bigger decision, like whether a new entity actually makes sense before you file the paperwork.

This is where the Team of Three model earns its keep. A single generalist bookkeeper can maintain one entity's books. Keeping multiple entities, multiple states, and multiple filing deadlines aligned takes three people paying attention to three different layers of the business.

## Technology and workflows tailored to you

No two clients run on the same software, so Aurora Consulting Group doesn't force one. We build a technology stack around the systems you already use, whether that's Gusto for payroll, a point-of-sale platform for a retail client, or project software for a contractor tracking job costs.

Every client gets a secure digital workflow for sharing documents, approving payroll, and reviewing financials, built so information moves without a stack of email attachments or a shared drive nobody can find. As a Gusto Partner, we set up payroll integrations that sync directly with your books rather than requiring a manual re-entry every pay period.

The result is a system that fits how your business already operates, not one that asks you to change how you work to fit ours.

## Fixed pricing, no surprises

Hourly billing creates an incentive to work slowly and a habit of dreading the invoice. Aurora Consulting Group prices every engagement as a fixed monthly fee, agreed on before work begins.

- One flat monthly rate covering your Team of Three, not a running meter
- No annual contract locking you into a service that no longer fits
- Pricing scaled to the complexity of your business, not padded for unpredictability
- Clear scope set upfront, so a phone call doesn't turn into a surprise line item

You'll know the cost of working with us before you sign anything, and you're free to leave if the fit changes. That's the kind of transparency we'd want from an advisor of our own.
