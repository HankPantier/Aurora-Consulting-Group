## Why accountants choose Aurora for their next career move

Most accounting jobs put you in one lane and keep you there. Bookkeeping. Tax prep. Compliance. You do the same task on repeat, for years, and call it a career.

Aurora built something different. Our Team of 3 model means every client gets a Client Service Manager, a Client Controller, and a Client CFO working together, and every team member gets exposure to work across that full spectrum. You'll touch monthly close, strategic tax planning, and advisory conversations with business owners, not just one slice of the job.

We're fully remote and headquartered in Visalia, California, with team members supporting clients across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties and beyond. No commute, no cubicle, no corporate hierarchy standing between you and the client relationship. If you want to grow past a single job title, this is where that happens.

<!-- block: service-cards | variant: 3-col -->

## Open roles and the client work you'll do

We hire for real client work, not generic job descriptions. Here's what a few of our roles actually look like day to day.

### Staff Accountant
icon: Calculator

Handles monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, and reporting for a mix of clients, from real estate investors managing depreciation schedules to contractors tracking job costs across active projects.

### Client Controller
icon: ChartBar

Oversees accounting operations for a client portfolio, catches issues before they become problems, and works closely with business-to-business service providers on billable versus overhead tracking.

### Tax Strategist
icon: FileCheck

Builds proactive tax plans for entrepreneurs and multi-entity businesses, including entity structure work for real estate investors and quarterly estimate planning for commission-based real estate brokers.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: teammate-integrity-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant reviewing client documents carefully at a desk" | query: "accountant reviewing documents attentive" -->

## What we look for in a teammate

We're not looking for someone who just wants to close tickets. We're looking for people who care whether the numbers are actually right, and who tell a client the truth even when it's not the easy answer.

- Integrity with financial data, even under deadline pressure
- Comfort learning new software fast, since every client runs on a customized tech stack
- Genuine interest in advisory conversations, not just transactional data entry
- Responsiveness that matches a business owner's pace, not a 9-to-5 clock
- A collaborative streak, since you'll work inside a three-person client team

If you'd rather sit quietly and process transactions than talk strategy with a client, this probably isn't the right fit. If you want to be the person a business owner calls before they make a big decision, keep reading.

## How to apply

### Send us your resume
Upload your resume and tell us which role interests you. It takes about five minutes.

### Have a real conversation
If your background fits, someone from our team calls you directly, not a bot, not a recruiter reading a script.

### Meet the people you'd work with
We introduce you to the actual team you'd join, because you should know who you're working with before you say yes.

### Get an answer, fast
We aim to respond within a few business days at every step. No black hole applications here.

If you'd rather talk before applying, you can [schedule a consultation](/contact) with our team and ask questions first.
