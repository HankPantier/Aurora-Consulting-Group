## Advisory that goes beyond bookkeeping for real estate investors

Most real estate investors don't need another bookkeeper. They need someone asking the harder questions: should this next property go into a new LLC, is your depreciation schedule actually saving you money this year, and what happens to your cash position if a refinance falls through? Aurora Consulting Group's Advisory & Coaching service is built around those questions, not just year-end filings.

Every client works with a Team of 3, a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, delivering the combined expertise of three professionals for less than the cost of one full-time hire. For an investor managing several properties and entities across Central California, that structure means someone is reviewing your numbers between tax seasons, not just before them. It's advisory built for people who plan to keep growing.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: coaching-strategy-session.jpg | alt: "Advisor and real estate investor reviewing portfolio strategy at a desk" | query: "business advisor client meeting discussion" -->
## Two coaching tracks for financial strategy and business growth

Aurora's advisory model runs on two complementary tracks. The first is financial strategy coaching, focused on tax positioning, entity structure, and reinvestment planning, the kind of decisions that determine whether a 1031 exchange or a cost segregation study actually pays off. The second is business coaching, aimed at the operational side: staffing decisions, systems, and the growth plan behind the portfolio itself.

For investors confused about whether a property belongs in an LLC, an S-corp, or a straightforward personal hold, the financial strategy track brings clarity before the closing happens, not after. The business coaching track picks up from there, helping owners decide when to add a property manager, when to bring on staff, and how to reinvest profit without straining cash flow. Together, the two tracks give investors a full picture instead of a partial one.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: depreciation-cost-segregation-review.jpg | alt: "Advisor reviewing property depreciation schedules with an investor client" | query: "accountant reviewing property financial documents" -->
## Solving the challenges unique to real estate investing

Real estate investors face a specific set of accounting problems that generic bookkeeping software doesn't solve. Aurora's advisory team works directly on these, including:

- Depreciation schedules and cost segregation tracking maintained across every property in your portfolio, not recalculated from scratch each year
- Entity structure guidance for LLCs, S-corps, and holding company setups, matched to how you actually plan to grow
- Cash flow visibility across a growing portfolio, so you know which properties are carrying the group and which are draining it
- Tax strategy built around acquisition timing, refinancing, and disposition, not just the April deadline

These are the questions that come up in a real advisory meeting, not a generic year-end call. Because the Team of 3 model puts a Client CFO on your account, portfolio-level strategy gets the same attention as the day-to-day books.

## Guidance for multi-state and multi-entity portfolios

Investors expanding beyond California run into a different tier of complexity: state registration requirements, nexus questions, and nexus and compliance obligations that shift with every new jurisdiction. Add a second or third entity, and intercompany transactions start needing consolidated reporting that a single-entity bookkeeping setup was never built to handle.

Aurora's advisory clients with multi-state and multi-entity holdings meet with their team more frequently than a typical annual check-in, because these are decisions that shouldn't wait. Your Client Controller tracks the entity-level detail, while your Client CFO looks at the consolidated picture, so registration deadlines and reporting obligations don't get discovered after the fact. If your portfolio spans Tulare, Kings, Fresno, or Kern County and beyond, that structure is designed to scale with you.
