## Advisory built around your industry

Every business we serve runs differently. A real estate investor tracks depreciation across a dozen properties; a nonprofit tracks grant restrictions across a dozen funding sources. Generic bookkeeping doesn't hold up in either case.

That's why Aurora Consulting Group builds each engagement around a Team of 3, a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, who bring the expertise of three specialists for less than the cost of one full-time hire. Fixed monthly pricing, no annual contracts, and a technology stack customized to your workflow keep the relationship flexible as your business grows.

Whether you manage rental properties in Tulare County or run a multi-entity company with operations in several states, the accounting and advisory should match how you actually work. Here's how we approach the industries we know best.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: real-estate-investor-review.jpg | alt: "Real estate investor reviewing property financial documents with an advisor" | query: "real estate investor reviewing documents" -->
## Real estate investors

Real estate investors rarely lose money on rent. They lose it on tax strategy nobody caught in time. Depreciation schedules across multiple properties, cost segregation studies, and the constant question of LLC versus S-corp structuring can turn a profitable portfolio into a compliance headache.

Our Client CFOs build depreciation tracking into the monthly close, not just the tax return, so cost segregation opportunities get flagged while they still matter. We help structure entities to match how you actually hold and finance property, then keep that structure current as you acquire or sell.

If your portfolio spans Visalia, Fresno County, or beyond, [our real estate investor services](/industries/real-estate-investors) are built around exactly this kind of complexity.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: real-estate-broker-commission.jpg | alt: "Real estate broker reviewing commission statements at a desk" | query: "real estate broker reviewing paperwork" -->
## Real estate brokers

Commission income doesn't arrive on a schedule, and that unpredictability makes tax planning harder, not optional. A broker who closes three deals in March and none in April needs a financial team that plans around that rhythm, not one that panics every April 15.

Aurora Consulting Group builds quarterly estimated payment plans around your actual closing calendar, factors in self-employment tax from the first conversation, and keeps a cash reserve strategy in place so slow months don't become a crisis. Enrolled Agents on our team can represent you directly with the IRS if a question ever comes up.

Brokers across Tulare, Kings, and Kern counties use [our brokerage-focused services](/industries/real-estate-brokers) to keep commission income working for them year-round.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: contractor-job-site-review.jpg | alt: "Contractor reviewing project costs on a tablet at a job site" | query: "contractor reviewing project budget jobsite" -->
## Contractors and trades

Job costing determines whether a contractor is actually profitable or just busy. Without profitability tracking on each active job, it's easy to keep working while margins quietly disappear.

Our team builds job costing directly into your accounting so you know profitability by project, not just by the year:

- Job costing and profitability tracking across every active project
- Payroll setup and subcontractor 1099 compliance handled correctly the first time
- Cash flow forecasting that accounts for retainage and progress billing
- Entity and tax structure reviews as your bonding capacity and revenue grow

Contractors working across Central California rely on [our contractor accounting services](/industries/contractors) to keep the books as organized as the job site.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: consultant-technology-workspace.jpg | alt: "Consultant and technology founder reviewing billing reports on a laptop" | query: "consultant reviewing reports laptop office" -->
## Professionals, consultants, and technology companies

Attorneys, physicians, architects, and consultants share a problem most bookkeeping software ignores: knowing which hours and expenses are billable and which are overhead. SaaS founders and e-commerce operators face a version of the same issue, tracking cost of revenue against recurring revenue as the business scales.

We set up your chart of accounts and reporting to separate billable engagement costs from overhead automatically, so profitability by client or by product line is visible every month, not just at tax time. As firms and startups grow past the point where a bookkeeper alone can keep up, our Client Controller and Client CFO layer in the forecasting and cash management that growth requires.

Legal, medical, engineering, architecture, real estate, and healthcare professionals, along with technology companies from early-stage startups to established platforms, work with [our business services team](/industries/business-to-business-service-providers) for back-office support that scales with them.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: multi-entity-financial-reporting.jpg | alt: "Financial team reviewing consolidated reports for multiple business entities" | query: "team reviewing financial reports office" -->
## Multi-state and multi-entity businesses

Operating in more than one state or through more than one entity multiplies the compliance calendar fast. State registration deadlines, nexus questions, and consolidated reporting requirements pile up in ways a single-state business never has to think about.

We track registration and filing obligations across every jurisdiction where you operate, reconcile intercompany transactions between entities, and produce consolidated reporting your lenders and investors can actually use:

- State registration and nexus monitoring across jurisdictions
- Intercompany transaction tracking and elimination for consolidated reporting
- Entity-level tax planning coordinated across the whole structure
- One embedded advisory team instead of separate firms per entity

Businesses with operations across California and beyond use [our multi-entity services](/industries/multi-state-multi-entity-businesses) to keep every entity in sync.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: nonprofit-board-meeting-review.jpg | alt: "Nonprofit executive director reviewing budget reports with board members" | query: "nonprofit board meeting reviewing budget" -->
## Nonprofits and mission-driven organizations

Boards and executive directors carry a different kind of accountability. Grant restrictions, functional expense reporting, and donor trust all depend on financial statements a board member without an accounting background can still understand.

Aurora Consulting Group has worked directly with association and nonprofit leadership, including the consulting relationship highlighted in the Moorland Association News Survey feature on the firm, to build reporting that satisfies auditors and board members alike. Advisory and coaching sessions built for nonprofit leadership cover budget-to-actual variance, grant compliance, and the operational questions that come up between board meetings.

If your organization needs a financial team that speaks board language and compliance language equally well, [our advisory and coaching services](/services/advisory-coaching) extend that support beyond the annual audit.
