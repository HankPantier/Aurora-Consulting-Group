## Accounting support built for owners who'd rather run their business

Running a business in Visalia, or anywhere across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, or Kern County, means your attention belongs with customers, not with categorizing expenses in QuickBooks at 11pm. Aurora Consulting Group's outsourced accounting service handles the books so you can handle the business.

Full bookkeeping, reconciliations, and reporting arrive on a schedule you can plan around, delivered by people who treat your numbers like their own. This isn't a call center reading from a script. It's a small, culture-driven group of accountants who work remotely and stay close to your business year-round, month after month.

Your team for tax and accounting, and a whole lot more, starts here.

## Meet your team of three financial experts

Most outsourced bookkeeping arrangements hand you a single generalist and hope for the best. Aurora structures every engagement around three specialists, each with a distinct role, so nothing falls through the cracks between bookkeeping, strategy, and communication.

### Client Service Manager
icon: UserCheck

Your main point of contact, coordinating deadlines, questions, and day-to-day communication so you're never chasing down an answer.

### Client Controller
icon: FileCheck

Oversees the books, reconciliations, and monthly financial reporting, catching discrepancies before they become real problems.

### Client CFO
icon: ChartLine

Brings strategic insight to cash flow, budgeting, and growth decisions, the kind of guidance usually reserved for a full-time hire.

Together, that's the expertise of three professionals for less than the cost of one full-time employee.

## What's included in your bookkeeping and accounting plan

Every plan starts with the fundamentals done right, then builds out based on how your business actually operates.

- Daily and monthly bookkeeping, categorized and reconciled against your bank and credit card statements
- Financial statement preparation, including profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow reports
- Payroll coordination through Gusto, keeping wages, tax deposits, and filings on schedule, backed by Aurora's status as a listed Gusto Partner
- QuickBooks setup, cleanup, and ongoing management, built around your existing workflow rather than forcing a switch
- Secure digital document exchange and reporting, so sensitive financial data never travels by unsecured email
- A technology stack chosen for your business, not a one-size-fits-all system applied to every client the same way

Each piece connects back to your Client Controller, who reviews the numbers before they ever reach you.

## Tax planning and IRS representation from Enrolled Agents

Tax prep once a year tells you what already happened. Aurora builds tax strategy into the accounting relationship year-round, adjusting as your income, entity structure, or expenses shift.

Enrolled Agent status carries IRS representation authority. If a notice arrives, an audit gets scheduled, or a collections issue needs resolving, your Client CFO or Client Controller can speak to the IRS directly on your behalf, not just help you draft a response and hope for the best.

For owners across Visalia and Central California managing multiple entities or multi-state operations, that combination of proactive planning and representation authority closes a gap that bookkeeping-only services tend to leave open. It's why tax planning and accounting live under one roof at Aurora instead of two separate vendors.

## Simple, fixed monthly pricing with no surprises

Aurora charges one flat monthly fee, agreed on before work begins, based on the complexity of your business rather than hours logged. No annual contract locks you in, and no surprise invoice shows up after a busy quarter.

Pricing scales with real factors: number of entities, transaction volume, payroll headcount, and how many states you operate in. A single-entity consulting practice in Visalia pays differently than a multi-entity nonprofit with locations across Tulare and Kings County, and both know their number before day one.

If your needs change, your plan changes with them, discussed openly at your next advisory meeting rather than buried in fine print months later.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Bookkeeping, Tax & QuickBooks Experts | Aurora Consulting Group

**Q: What does outsourced bookkeeping with Aurora Consulting Group include?**
A: Your plan covers daily bookkeeping, bank and credit card reconciliations, monthly financial statements, payroll coordination through Gusto, and QuickBooks setup or cleanup, all managed by a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO on a technology stack built for your business.

**Q: How is Aurora's pricing structured?**
A: Aurora charges one fixed monthly fee based on your business complexity, transaction volume, entity count, and payroll size, agreed on before work starts. There's no annual contract, so pricing adjusts as your business changes without locking you into a long-term commitment.

**Q: Can Aurora represent my business if the IRS sends a notice?**
A: Yes. Aurora's Enrolled Agents hold IRS representation authority, meaning they can communicate directly with the IRS on audits, notices, and collections matters on your behalf, rather than simply helping you prepare a response yourself.

**Q: Does Aurora work with businesses operating in multiple states?**
A: Multi-state and multi-entity operations are a core focus. Aurora coordinates state registrations, nexus tracking, intercompany transactions, and consolidated reporting for clients across California and beyond, keeping every entity's compliance obligations organized in one place.
