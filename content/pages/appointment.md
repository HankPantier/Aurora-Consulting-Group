## Book time with our team in minutes
Scheduling a first conversation with Aurora Consulting Group takes less time than reading this page. Pick a slot that fits your calendar, and a member of our team will reach out to confirm the details. No sales pitch, no pressure to sign anything on the spot.

This first call is about understanding where your business stands today: your entity structure, your states of operation, the bottlenecks slowing you down. Whether you're a consultant juggling multiple client engagements, a SaaS founder expanding into new states, or a nonprofit board member trying to make sense of grant reporting, we start by listening.

Grab your calendar. The rest takes care of itself.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: intro-call-client-service-manager.jpg | alt: "Client service manager speaking with a business owner on a video call" | query: "professional video call consultation office" -->
## What to expect from your first conversation
Your Client Service Manager leads the call, typically 30 to 45 minutes. They'll ask about your business structure, current bookkeeping setup, and the specific problems pulling your attention away from running things day to day. If multi-state registration, payroll compliance, or entity restructuring comes up, they'll flag it for deeper follow-up with your future Client Controller and Client CFO.

Expect direct questions and honest answers, not a scripted sales presentation. If Aurora isn't the right fit for what you need, we'll say so plainly and point you toward something that will help. If it is a fit, you'll leave with a clear next step: a proposal outline, a document request list, or a second meeting with the team member suited to your situation.

No recordings to sit through later, no intake form to fill out blind.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: business-owner-preparing-documents.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing financial documents before a consultation call" | query: "small business owner reviewing paperwork" -->
## Come prepared to talk about your business
A little preparation turns 30 minutes into a genuinely useful conversation. You don't need polished financials or a formal presentation, just enough detail so we can ask the right follow-up questions.

Before the call, have a rough sense of:

- Your entity structure (LLC, S-corp, multiple entities) and how many states you operate in
- Your current bookkeeping setup, whether that's a spreadsheet, QuickBooks, or nothing consistent yet
- The specific pain point pulling you here: compliance deadlines, cash flow gaps, payroll headaches, or a growing to-do list with no one to hand it to
- Any upcoming triggers, a new state registration, an acquisition, a funding round, that's adding urgency

If you're not sure about any of this, that's fine too. Part of the first call is helping you figure out what you don't know yet.
