## A team of three, working as one

Most accounting firms hand you a single point of contact and call it service. Aurora Consulting Group built something more useful: every client works with a Client Service Manager, a Client Controller, and a Client CFO, three specialists assigned to one account. We call it the Team of 3.

Each role covers different ground. The Client Service Manager handles day-to-day questions and keeps communication moving. The Client Controller manages the accuracy of your books, from reconciliations to financial statements. The Client CFO brings the strategic view: tax planning, cash flow forecasting, and growth guidance. Together they deliver the expertise of three professionals for less than the cost of one full-time hire, without the turnover risk of building an in-house finance department from scratch.

## Outsourced accounting that keeps your books audit-ready

Bookkeeping shouldn't be something you piece together every March. Aurora Consulting Group handles full-service bookkeeping and back-office accounting year-round, so the numbers are ready whenever you, a lender, or an auditor need them.

- Monthly reconciliations and financial statements delivered on a consistent schedule
- A technology stack built around how your business actually operates, including Gusto for payroll, not a generic software bundle
- Secure digital workflows for document exchange, approvals, and reporting
- Consolidated reporting across multiple entities, built for multi-state operations and growing technology or e-commerce companies

For technology companies and multi-entity businesses juggling more than one set of books, that consolidated view often matters more than the bookkeeping itself. You get clean, current financials and time back to run the business.

## Tax planning and strategy built around your goals

Tax preparation looks backward. Tax planning looks forward, and that's the difference between filing a return in April and building a strategy that reduces what you owe all year. Aurora Consulting Group runs year-round tax planning built on your entity structure, income timing, and growth plans, not a once-a-year appointment.

Our team includes Enrolled Agents, credentialed by the IRS with the authority to represent clients directly before the agency. That matters when a notice arrives, an audit opens, or a multi-state filing requirement gets complicated. Instead of scrambling to find someone qualified to respond, you already have representation in place.

For businesses operating across state lines or through multiple entities, that authority extends into ongoing compliance work: state registrations, nexus questions, and coordinated filings that keep every jurisdiction current.

## Fixed pricing, no surprises

Hourly billing punishes you for asking questions. Aurora Consulting Group prices every engagement as a fixed monthly fee, agreed on upfront, with no annual contract locking you in.

- One flat monthly fee covering your full Team of 3
- No hourly surprises for a quick phone call or an extra question
- No long-term contract; review and adjust as your business changes
- Pricing scoped to your entity count, transaction volume, and service mix, not a generic tier

You know the cost before the year starts, and you can leave if the fit stops working. Most clients don't.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Services

**Q: What does the Team of 3 model include?**
A: Every client is assigned a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO. Together they cover day-to-day communication, bookkeeping accuracy, and strategic financial guidance, giving you three specialists for less than the cost of one full-time hire.

**Q: Does Aurora Consulting Group handle IRS audits or notices?**

**Q: How is pricing structured?**

**Q: Can Aurora Consulting Group support businesses with multiple entities or states?**
A: Yes. The firm handles consolidated reporting, state registrations, nexus tracking, and intercompany transactions for multi-state and multi-entity businesses, a common need among the technology companies and professional service firms it serves.
