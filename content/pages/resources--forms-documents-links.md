## Your resource library for organized, proactive accounting

Good advisory work starts before the meeting, not during it. The forms and checklists on this page give you a starting point to work from between calls with your Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, so the conversation stays focused on strategy instead of hunting for numbers.

This isn't a generic download dump. Every document here reflects the entity structures, deduction categories, and compliance questions we see most often in client files across Visalia, Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, and Kern County. Pull the resources that match your business, fill in what you can, and bring the rest to your next Team of 3 session. We'll take it from there.

## Real estate investor forms and deduction checklists

Depreciation gets complicated fast once a portfolio grows past one or two properties, and entity structure confusion (LLC versus S-corp, or a mix of both) can quietly cost investors thousands each year. These documents help you track basis, elections, and deductions property by property instead of guessing at tax time.

- Property-by-property depreciation schedule template
- Cost segregation study intake checklist
- LLC vs. S-corp entity comparison worksheet
- Rental property deduction checklist (repairs, mileage, home office)
- 1031 exchange documentation checklist
- Passive activity loss tracking log

If you're managing multiple properties or considering a restructure, our [real estate investor advisory work](/industries/real-estate-investors) goes deeper than any checklist can on its own.

## Contractor and job costing document templates

Job costing only works if the numbers get captured while the job is still open, not three months after the final invoice goes out. These templates are built for contractors juggling several active jobs at once, along with the payroll and subcontractor reporting that comes with them.

- Job costing tracker by project
- Subcontractor 1099 compliance checklist
- Certified payroll documentation checklist
- Equipment and mileage expense log
- Change order and progress billing worksheet
- Retainage tracking template

For a closer look at how job costing feeds into profitability reporting, visit our [contractor accounting page](/industries/contractors).

## Tax planning worksheets for brokers and B2B service providers

Commission cycles and client billing cycles create the same underlying problem: income that arrives unevenly but taxes that are due on a fixed schedule. Brokers need a way to plan quarterly payments around commission timing, while B2B firms need to see which hours and expenses are billable versus overhead.

- Quarterly estimated tax payment worksheet for commission income
- Self-employment tax planning checklist for brokers
- Billable vs. overhead expense allocation template
- Client engagement profitability tracker
- 1099 income reconciliation worksheet
- Home office and vehicle deduction checklist

Brokers can find more on managing uneven income on our [real estate broker page](/industries/real-estate-brokers), and B2B firms can see how we track engagement profitability on our [B2B service provider page](/industries/business-to-business-service-providers).

## Multi-state and multi-entity compliance resources

Operating across state lines or through more than one entity adds registration, nexus, and reporting obligations that a single-entity business never has to think about. These resources help you keep track of where you're registered, how entities transact with each other, and when consolidated numbers need to roll up.

- State nexus and registration checklist
- Multi-entity chart of accounts template
- Intercompany transaction tracking log
- Consolidated reporting worksheet
- Franchise tax and annual report due-date calendar
- Foreign qualification document checklist

Multi-state and multi-entity structures are where our advisory role tends to run deepest. See how that plays out on our [multi-state and multi-entity businesses page](/industries/multi-state-multi-entity-businesses).

## How to put these documents to work with your Team of 3

A checklist tells you what's missing. It doesn't tell you what to do about it, and that's where your Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO come in. Bring a filled-in worksheet to your next call and they'll turn it into a plan: a restructure, a quarterly payment strategy, a corrected chart of accounts, whatever the numbers point to.

Most clients get more value from these documents in the context of a fixed-monthly relationship than they would trying to piece together answers on their own. If you'd like a second set of eyes on any of the forms above, [schedule a consultation](/contact) and we'll walk through them together.
