## Overview

Running a business in Visalia, Fresno, or anywhere across Central California means juggling tax deadlines, payroll changes, and financial decisions that shift year over year. This is where our Insights hub comes in: practical, plain-language guidance from the same Enrolled Agents and advisors who work inside your books every month.

Aurora Consulting Group's Insights section covers what actually matters for entrepreneurs, professionals, and multi-entity businesses: quarterly tax planning moves, HR and compliance updates, cash flow strategies for service-based firms, and accounting practices built for technology companies and nonprofits. Every article reflects the same Team of 3 approach clients rely on for their books, so the guidance you read here is the same guidance we're giving in client meetings that week.

This hub gets updated as tax law changes, IRS deadlines shift, and new compliance requirements hit California businesses. A contractor managing job costing across three active projects needs different information than a nonprofit board preparing for a 990 filing, or a SaaS founder tracking deferred revenue across two entities. You'll find both broad strategy pieces and narrow, niche-specific breakdowns here, organized so you can find what applies to your situation without wading through generic advice.

None of this replaces a conversation about your specific numbers. Think of Insights as the starting point, the read that helps you ask sharper questions when you sit down with your Client CFO. If something here raises a question about your filing status, entity structure, or multi-state exposure, that's exactly the kind of conversation our advisory meetings are built for. Browse by topic, bookmark what's relevant to your industry, and reach out when you're ready to talk through how it applies to your business.
