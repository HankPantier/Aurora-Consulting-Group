## A team built around your business, not a single accountant

Most small businesses end up with one overworked bookkeeper or a single CPA splitting attention across too many files. Aurora Consulting Group built something different. Every client gets a dedicated Team of 3: a Client Service Manager, a Client Controller, and a Client CFO.

Your Client Service Manager keeps day-to-day questions answered and deadlines on track, so nothing falls through the cracks between tax season and year-end. Your Client Controller owns the accounting detail: reconciliations, reporting, and the numbers behind your numbers. Your Client CFO brings the strategic view, tax planning, cash flow forecasting, and the advisory conversations that help you decide what to do next, not just what already happened.

This structure gives you three professionals' worth of expertise for less than the cost of one full-time hire. There's no single point of failure and no waiting on one person's calendar. It's a coordinated team that knows your business file as well as you do, because three people are actually looking at it.

## Enrolled agents who can represent you before the IRS

An IRS letter shouldn't mean scrambling to find someone who can actually speak to the agency on your behalf. Enrolled Agents on the Aurora Consulting Group team hold federal authorization to represent clients directly before the IRS, on audits, back tax matters, and multi-state notices alike.

That credential matters most when things get complicated: a missed filing across two states, a residency question after a move, or an audit notice that lands in your mailbox with no warning. Instead of forwarding you to outside counsel, our EAs handle the correspondence, the documentation, and the conversation.

- Direct IRS representation authority, not just tax preparation
- Support for audits, back taxes, and penalty abatement requests
- Multi-state notice and compliance handling for clients operating across jurisdictions
- Proactive planning that reduces the odds of a notice in the first place

## A culture of responsiveness and genuine partnership

Aurora Consulting Group operates fully remote and built the culture to match: fast, direct, and human. Team members are chosen for how they communicate, not just what they know on paper. That shows up in how quickly you get an answer and how little you have to repeat yourself.

We treat every engagement as an embedded extension of your business rather than an outside vendor filing paperwork once a year. Frequent advisory meetings are built into the relationship, not billed as an upsell. If your business runs across multiple states or entities and needs HR, corporate compliance, or insurance questions routed to someone who already knows your file, that's the working relationship this team is designed for.

## Rooted in Central California, built for growth anywhere

Aurora Consulting Group was founded in Visalia, California, and most of the client base is based across Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, Kern County, and the surrounding Central California region. That local footprint still shapes how the firm works: direct relationships, fast response times, and a real understanding of the agricultural, real estate, and small business economies here.

The fully remote structure also means the team serves clients with operations well beyond those five counties. Multi-state and multi-entity businesses, technology companies, and professional practices outside California work with the same Team of 3 model, the same secure digital workflows, and the same fixed monthly pricing, regardless of zip code.
