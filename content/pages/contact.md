## What happens after you reach out

### We respond within one business day
A real person reads every inquiry, and most get a reply the same day. No auto-responder pretending to be a person, no three-week wait to find out if we even take new clients.

### A no-pressure discovery call
We spend 20 to 30 minutes learning your business, your entity structure, and where your books or your tax strategy are falling short. You leave the call with clarity, whether or not we end up working together.

### A fixed monthly proposal, no annual contract
If it's a fit, you'll get a proposal with fixed monthly pricing built around your Team of 3, tied to the work your business actually needs. No long-term lock-in, no surprise hourly billing.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Contact Us | Aurora Consulting Group LLC

**Q: How much does Aurora Consulting Group charge?**
A: Pricing is a fixed monthly fee based on the scope of your accounting, tax, and advisory needs, not hourly billing. There are no annual contracts, so the fee can adjust as your business grows or your needs change.

**Q: Do you only work with California businesses?**
A: Most clients are based in Visalia, Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties, but the firm works remotely with multi-state and multi-entity businesses across the country, using secure digital workflows built for each client.

**Q: Can you represent my business in front of the IRS?**
A: Yes. Enrolled Agents on the team hold IRS representation authority, which means they can communicate directly with the IRS on your behalf during an audit, notice, or collections matter.

**Q: What industries do you specialize in?**
A: The firm focuses on professionals and consultants, technology companies, nonprofits, real estate investors and brokers, contractors, business-to-business service providers, and multi-state or multi-entity businesses.

**Q: How fast will someone respond to my inquiry?**
A: Most inquiries get a reply within one business day from a real member of the team, followed by a no-pressure discovery call to talk through your entity structure and current pain points.
