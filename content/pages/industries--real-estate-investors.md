## Accounting built around how real estate investors operate

Real estate investors don't need generic bookkeeping. Portfolios grow property by property, and every acquisition carries its own depreciation schedule, loan structure, and tax treatment. Aurora Consulting Group builds accounting systems that track performance at the property level and the portfolio level at once, so an investor can see which assets carry their weight and which ones need attention.

Founded in Visalia, California, the firm works with investors holding rental properties, short-term rentals, and commercial assets across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties. Cap rates, cash-on-cash returns, and the difference between a 1031 exchange and a cost segregation study aren't abstractions here; they're the daily work. Large institutional firms manage funds and capital allocations for shareholders. This firm manages your books, your entities, and your tax strategy, built around the properties you actually hold.

## Depreciation and cost segregation tracking across every property

Missed depreciation is money left on the table, and it happens more than most investors realize once a portfolio grows past two or three properties. Aurora Consulting Group maintains depreciation schedules for every property individually and rolls them into a consolidated view, so nothing slips through when a property is bought, improved, or sold.

What's included for each property in your portfolio:

- Depreciation schedules tracked from acquisition through disposition, including component-level detail
- Coordination with cost segregation studies to accelerate depreciation on qualifying components
- Adjustments for capital improvements, partial dispositions, and bonus depreciation eligibility
- Reconciliation against tax filings each year so schedules and returns always match

This level of tracking is what separates a firm that files your return once a year from one that manages the asset with you.

## Choosing the right entity structure for your portfolio

Most investors start with a single LLC and end up confused three properties later. Should each property sit in its own LLC? Does an S-corp election make sense for a flipping business but not a buy-and-hold portfolio? Does a holding company reduce liability exposure without adding unnecessary complexity? These questions get harder, not easier, as a portfolio grows.

Aurora Consulting Group's Team of 3 model puts a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO on your structure decisions together, so liability protection and tax efficiency get weighed at the same time, not in separate conversations with separate advisors. The right structure at five properties is rarely the right structure at fifteen. This team revisits entity structure as the portfolio changes, rather than setting it once and moving on.

## Proactive tax planning for property acquisitions and sales

Tax strategy for real estate investors can't wait until March. A 1031 exchange has strict identification windows. A property sale mid-year changes estimated tax payments. An acquisition financed with a cash-out refinance carries interest tracing rules that affect what's deductible. Waiting until tax season to address any of these means the planning window has already closed.

Aurora Consulting Group's Enrolled Agents hold IRS representation authority and build tax strategy around your acquisition and disposition timeline throughout the year, not just at filing. Before a sale closes, before a refinance funds, before a new property enters an entity, the tax impact gets modeled and discussed. That's the difference between a preparer who reports what happened and a team that helps decide what happens next.

## Support for multi-state and multi-entity real estate portfolios

Investors who expand beyond one county, or one state, run into compliance questions that a single-property owner never faces. State registration requirements differ. Nexus rules determine where you owe tax, not just where the property sits. Consolidated reporting across multiple entities gets complicated fast when properties and entities don't share a structure.

Aurora Consulting Group supports investors managing:

- State registration and annual compliance filings across jurisdictions
- Nexus determination as portfolios expand beyond California into new states
- Intercompany transactions and consolidated reporting across multiple LLCs or holding entities
- Coordinated tax filings so no state deadline or entity return gets missed

For investors holding properties across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties and beyond, this coordination happens inside one team instead of across several disconnected advisors.
