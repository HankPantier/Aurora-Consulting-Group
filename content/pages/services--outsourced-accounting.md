## A full back office without a full-time hire

Hiring one in-house bookkeeper costs more than the salary line suggests once payroll taxes, benefits, software, and training enter the picture. And one person covers one skill set, usually data entry, not financial strategy.

Outsourced accounting through Aurora Consulting Group gives you a full back office: bookkeeping, reconciliations, financial reporting, and strategic guidance, without adding a line to your payroll. You get the expertise of three professionals for less than the cost of one full-time hire. That's the entire premise behind our Team of 3 model. A business past a million dollars in revenue needs more than someone entering transactions; it needs a controller catching errors before they compound and a CFO turning those numbers into decisions.

Whether you run a professional practice, a growing SaaS company, or a nonprofit answering to a board, the math holds: less overhead, more oversight.

<!-- block: feature-grid | variant: 3-col -->
## Meet your team of three

Every Aurora client is assigned three people, not one generalist juggling everything from data entry to tax strategy. Each role has a defined lane, and the three work together so nothing falls between them.

### Client Service Manager
icon: UserCheck

Your day-to-day contact. Handles transaction coding, bill pay, invoicing, and the questions that come up between monthly close, so nothing sits unanswered in an inbox for a week.

### Client Controller
icon: FileCheck

Oversees the books, reviews every reconciliation, and catches discrepancies before they become a surprise at tax time. It's the accuracy layer a single in-house hire rarely provides, since nobody audits their own work.

### Client CFO
icon: ChartLine

Reads the financial statements your controller prepares and turns them into direction: pricing decisions, cash flow forecasts, entity structure questions, and the calls that shape where your business goes next.

This structure is also why the model pencils out financially. You're paying for three specialized functions, priced together, for less than one full-time controller salary alone. Curious how the CFO layer works day to day? See [Advisory & Coaching](/services/advisory-coaching) for how those conversations run alongside the accounting.

## What outsourced accounting includes

The scope is built around what a professional services firm, a technology company, or a multi-entity operation actually needs each month, not a generic bookkeeping package.

- Monthly bookkeeping and transaction categorization across every business account
- Accounts payable and accounts receivable management, including vendor payments and client invoicing
- Bank and credit card reconciliations completed on a set monthly schedule
- Financial statement preparation: profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
- Monthly or quarterly reporting calls, sized to how often your business needs to talk
- A reporting cadence built around your entity structure and industry, not a one-size template

Clients in professional services, technology, and nonprofit work also lean on us for the pieces that sit next to accounting: HR questions, corporate compliance deadlines, and insurance coordination that a solo bookkeeper typically can't touch.

## Built for multi-state and multi-entity complexity

One entity in one state is manageable with basic bookkeeping software. Three entities across California and two other states, with intercompany loans and a consolidated P&L your board wants to see, is a different problem.

We track nexus and registration obligations by jurisdiction, reconcile intercompany transactions so they don't distort each entity's standalone numbers, and produce consolidated reporting that shows the whole business alongside each piece of it. This is where the Client Controller and Client CFO roles matter most: someone has to keep the entities separate on paper while still telling you how the group performs together. If this describes your operation, our [Multi-State and Multi-Entity Businesses](/industries/multi-state-multi-entity-businesses) page goes into more detail on how we structure that work.

## Technology and workflows customized to how you work

We don't hand every client the same software stack and call it done. Your Client Service Manager sets up a digital workflow built around the tools you already use, or recommends new ones where it makes sense, then keeps everything running through secure, encrypted channels.

For clients running payroll, we work directly inside Gusto as a listed Gusto Partner, syncing payroll data with your books instead of re-entering it by hand. The result is a system specific to your business: your entities, your reporting needs, your existing software, connected in one place instead of scattered across five logins.

## Simple, fixed pricing with no long-term contract

You'll know your monthly cost before the engagement starts, and it won't move because your invoice volume had a busy quarter. That's fixed monthly pricing, set based on the actual complexity of your business, not a per-transaction meter running in the background.

There's no annual contract locking you in. If the fit isn't right, you're not stuck for twelve months finding that out. Most clients stay because the Team of 3 model earns its cost back in caught errors, better cash flow visibility, and CFO-level input they weren't getting before, not because they signed something that makes leaving expensive.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Outsourced Accounting

**Q: What is included in outsourced accounting services?**
A: Outsourced accounting typically includes monthly bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable management, bank reconciliations, and financial statement preparation. **Q: How much does outsourced accounting cost compared to hiring in-house?**
A: Aurora Consulting Group prices outsourced accounting on a fixed monthly basis, generally less than the fully loaded cost of one full-time hire once payroll taxes, benefits, and software are factored in. There's no annual contract, so pricing stays predictable without a long-term lock-in.

**Q: Can outsourced accounting handle multiple entities or states?**
A: Yes. Aurora Consulting Group's Client Controller and Client CFO roles are built to track intercompany transactions, manage state-by-state nexus and registration obligations, and produce consolidated reporting across entities, a common need for multi-state and multi-entity clients.

**Q: What software does Aurora Consulting Group use for outsourced accounting?**
A: The technology stack is customized per client rather than standardized. As a listed Gusto Partner, Aurora integrates payroll data directly into your books, and secure digital workflows are set up around whatever accounting software your business already runs on.
