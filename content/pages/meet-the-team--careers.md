## Why build your career with our team

Most accounting jobs ask you to process transactions and move on. At Aurora Consulting Group, the work is different because the relationship is different. We built a Team of 3 model, pairing a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO around every client, so the people doing the work actually understand the business behind the numbers.

If you're tired of compliance-only checklists and want to be part of strategic conversations, this is a place where that happens weekly, not once a year at tax time. Founded in Visalia, California, we work with entrepreneurs, professionals, and multi-entity businesses across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties who expect proactive answers, not year-end surprises.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: remote-workspace-accountant.jpg | alt: "Accountant working from a home office desk with laptop and notebook" | query: "home office professional working laptop" -->
## What it's like to work here

The firm runs fully remote, with a culture-driven team that stays connected through structured check-ins rather than open floor plans. You'll work inside the Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO structure, which means clear ownership of your role and daily visibility into how your work fits the bigger picture for each client.

There's no corporate script here. Conversations with clients and coworkers are direct, and feedback moves fast because the team is small enough that everyone knows the accounts they touch. Clients get fixed monthly pricing and no annual contracts, so the team's focus stays on delivering value, not defending a scope-of-work document. That accountability shapes how the whole team operates, including how new hires are trained and supported.

## Roles we're building toward

As Aurora grows its base of multi-state and multi-entity clients, we're adding capacity across all three service lines: outsourced accounting, advisory and coaching, and tax planning and strategy.

### Client Controller track
icon: Calculator

Hands-on bookkeeping and financial statement review for clients across real estate, professional services, and technology sectors. Strong candidates bring QuickBooks or similar platform experience and comfort managing multiple client books at once.

### Client CFO track
icon: ChartLine

Strategic advisory work: cash flow forecasting, budgeting, and the kind of forward-looking analysis that turns a bookkeeping relationship into a growth partnership. Ideal for candidates who want client-facing advisory responsibility, not just data entry.

### Enrolled Agent and tax strategist track
icon: ShieldCheck

Year-round tax planning and IRS representation for entrepreneurs and multi-entity businesses. Enrolled Agent credentials or active pursuit of EA status is a strong fit here, especially for candidates interested in multi-state compliance work.

### Client Service Manager track
icon: Users

The relationship hub for each client account, coordinating between the Controller and CFO roles while managing communication, deadlines, and onboarding for new engagements across our California client base.

## Who thrives on our team

The candidates who do well here share a few traits, regardless of which track they join.

- Comfortable building relationships with business owners, not just processing their transactions
- Detail-oriented enough to catch a depreciation schedule error or a misclassified 1099 before it becomes a problem
- Curious about the businesses behind the numbers, whether that's a real estate investor with cost segregation questions or a SaaS founder tracking multi-state nexus
- Comfortable in a remote setting with structured communication instead of hallway check-ins
- Interested in serving professionals and consultants, nonprofits, contractors, and technology companies, since that's where most of our client base sits

## Growth and development opportunities

We treat credentials as career investments, not checkboxes. Team members pursuing Enrolled Agent status gain IRS representation authority, which opens direct client-facing tax work that larger firms often reserve for partners. Others build depth in advisory and coaching, developing the skills to run strategic planning sessions rather than just deliver reports.

Multi-state and multi-entity work is where the firm is growing fastest, so team members who develop expertise in state nexus rules, intercompany transactions, and consolidated reporting find themselves handling increasingly complex client relationships. Technology fluency matters too: each client runs on a customized stack, and comfort learning new platforms translates directly into career advancement.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Careers | Aurora Consulting Group LLC

**Q: Does Aurora Consulting Group hire remote accounting staff?**
A: Yes. The firm operates fully remote and hires across its Team of 3 model, Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO roles, along with Enrolled Agent and tax strategy positions supporting clients across Central California.

**Q: Do I need to be an Enrolled Agent to apply?**
A: No, but candidates actively pursuing Enrolled Agent status are strongly considered for the tax strategy track. EA credentials grant IRS representation authority, which is a core differentiator for the firm's tax planning services.

**Q: What locations does Aurora Consulting Group serve?**
A: The firm was founded in Visalia, California, and serves clients primarily across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties, with a growing base of multi-state and multi-entity businesses managed through a remote team structure.

**Q: What industries do team members work with?**
A: Team members support professionals and consultants, technology companies, nonprofits, real estate investors and brokers, contractors, and multi-state businesses, giving new hires exposure to varied client structures beyond routine bookkeeping.

**Q: How do I apply for a position?**
A: Send a resume and a short note indicating which track interests you, Client Controller, Client CFO, or Enrolled Agent, through our application page. The team responds personally rather than through automated hiring systems.
