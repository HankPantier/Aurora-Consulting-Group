## Our commitment to protecting your information

At Aurora Consulting Group LLC, we handle your financial details the same way we handle your tax return: carefully, accurately, and with a clear explanation of what's happening and why. Visalia is where we started, and the trust our clients place in us with sensitive numbers, bank records, and entity documents is something we take seriously every day. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and client relationships, how we use it, and the choices you have. If any of it seems unclear, our team is one phone call away and happy to walk through it in plain language, not legal jargon.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Information we collect

We collect a few categories of information depending on how you interact with Aurora Consulting Group. When you fill out a contact form or request a consultation, we collect your name, email, phone number, and business details you choose to share. Once you become a client, our onboarding process and secure digital workflows involve financial documents, tax records, payroll data, entity formation paperwork, and banking information necessary to deliver outsourced accounting, tax planning, and advisory services.

Our website also collects standard usage data: pages visited, browser type, and general location, gathered through analytics tools to help us understand how visitors use auroracgllc.com. We do not collect this information to build advertising profiles. If you're a real estate investor, contractor, broker, or B2B service provider sending us project or client-level financial data, that information is treated with the same confidentiality standards as any other client record.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## How we use your information

We use the information you share to do the work you've hired us for: outsourced accounting, tax planning and preparation, and advisory and coaching support. That includes preparing financial statements, filing returns, managing payroll compliance, and advising on multi-state or multi-entity structures. Contact information lets our Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO team stay in touch about deadlines, meetings, and deliverables.

We also use aggregated, non-identifying usage data to improve our website and refine the customized technology stacks we build for each client relationship. We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for purposes beyond delivering and improving the services you've engaged us for.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: secure-remote-workflow.jpg | alt: "Accountant working securely on a laptop in a home office setting" | query: "remote accountant laptop secure workflow" -->
## How we protect and store your data

Aurora Consulting Group operates as a fully remote, culture-driven team, and that structure comes with a real commitment to secure digital workflows. We use encrypted client portals, password-protected file sharing, and role-based access so that only the team members working on your account, your Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, can view your records.

For multi-state and multi-entity clients juggling registrations, nexus obligations, and intercompany transactions across jurisdictions, we keep entity-level records organized and access-controlled within the same secure systems. Financial documents are retained only as long as needed for the engagement or as required by IRS and state recordkeeping rules, then securely archived or deleted.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Sharing your information with third parties

We don't share your information with outside parties for marketing purposes, and we never sell client data. Limited sharing happens in specific, necessary circumstances. As Enrolled Agents with IRS representation authority, our team may communicate directly with the IRS or state tax agencies on your behalf when you've authorized us to do so.

We also work with technology partners, including Gusto for payroll processing, to deliver the customized systems each client relies on. Those partners access only the information required to perform their function. We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of our clients and team.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: client-privacy-rights.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing documents with an advisor at a desk" | query: "business owner reviewing documents advisor" -->
## Your privacy choices and rights

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold, request corrections, or ask us to delete data we no longer need for legal or engagement purposes. You can also opt out of non-essential email communications at any time.

- Request access to the personal information we have on file
- Ask us to correct inaccurate or outdated details
- Request deletion of information no longer required for compliance
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails while remaining on active client communications
- Ask questions about how your financial data moves through our systems

## Frequently Asked Questions About Privacy Policy | Aurora Consulting Group LLC

**Q: Does Aurora Consulting Group sell my personal information?**
A: No. Aurora Consulting Group LLC does not sell client or website visitor data to third parties. Information is used only to deliver outsourced accounting, tax planning, and advisory services, and to communicate with you about your engagement.

**Q: How does Aurora Consulting Group protect my financial data?**
A: We use encrypted client portals, password-protected file sharing, and role-based access limited to your assigned Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO, supporting our fully remote, secure digital workflow structure.

**Q: Can I request that my data be deleted?**
A: Yes. You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information no longer required for your engagement or for IRS and state recordkeeping obligations. Contact our team directly to submit a request.

**Q: Does Aurora Consulting Group share data with the IRS?**
A: Only when authorized. As Enrolled Agents with IRS representation authority, our team may communicate with the IRS or state agencies on your behalf when you've given us permission to represent you.
