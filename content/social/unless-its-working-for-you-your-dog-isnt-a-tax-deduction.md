## LinkedIn

A client's golden retriever greets every customer who walks into her Visalia boutique. He's a very good dog. He's still not a tax deduction.

The IRS draws a hard line here: under Publication 502, a trained service animal can qualify as a medical expense, subject to the 7.5% AGI floor. Under Publication 535, a guard dog protecting a contractor's equipment yard in Kern County can qualify as a business expense, if the work is documented. A family pet that spends time at the office qualifies for neither, no matter how much goodwill it generates.

This matters most for consultants, contractors, and multi-entity business owners who write off expenses without a paper trail, because that's exactly what an audit flags first.
