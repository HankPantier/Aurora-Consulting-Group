## LinkedIn

18 to 25 SaaS tools. That's the typical stack for the technology and B2B service companies we work with across Visalia, Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties, and it can run $1,500 to $3,000 a month, or $18,000 to $36,000 a year.

Most of that spend never gets reviewed with the same discipline as payroll or rent. A structured quarterly audit, matching every charge to a confirmed user, typically cuts 15% to 30% of recurring spend on the first pass.

For multi-entity businesses, the leak runs deeper: the same subscription can renew under three different entities before it shows up on a consolidated report.
