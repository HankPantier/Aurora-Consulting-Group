## LinkedIn

A CASA volunteer who drives to court hearings and family visits can deduct every one of those miles at 14 cents each, even though the hours in the courtroom don't count at all.

That's the split the IRS draws: your volunteer time is worth zero on a tax return, but the mileage, supplies, and travel you pay for out of pocket often qualify. A volunteer who drives 40 miles round trip twice a week delivering meals racks up about 4,000 miles a year, roughly a $560 deduction most people never claim.

This matters most for the attorneys, physicians, and consultants we work with across Tulare, Kings, and Fresno counties who sit on nonprofit boards. Run those costs through the wrong entity, or skip the mileage log, and the deduction disappears when it's questioned.
