## LinkedIn

A contractor running three job sites in Tulare County and a SaaS founder in Fresno could both open the same business credit card tomorrow. Only one of them would be using the right one.

Business credit card rewards are generally not taxable. The IRS treats cash back as a rebate on purchases, not income. Spend $50,000, earn $750 back, and that amount typically reduces your cost basis, not your revenue. Sign-up bonuses without a spending requirement can be treated differently, which is exactly the kind of gray area worth a conversation before you assume the answer.

The real opportunity is matching your card's categories to how your business actually spends, then reconciling redemptions in your books every month. For multi-state and multi-entity businesses splitting rewards across entities, that reconciliation isn't optional.
