## LinkedIn

Rent your second home for 14 days or fewer this year, and the IRS says you owe zero tax on that income. Cross 15 days, and the entire amount becomes taxable, and you're now splitting expenses between personal and rental use. For Central California owners with a cabin near Kern County or a place outside Visalia, this single number decides whether you keep full mortgage interest and property tax deductions or shift into mixed-use reporting with loss limitations attached. Most owners don't find out which category they're in until tax season, after the decision is already made. Our tax planning team runs this projection with clients across Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern counties before the property closes, not after.
