## LinkedIn

A real estate broker driving 15,000 miles a year between showings can blow past a lease's 10,000 to 12,000 mile cap fast. Penalties run 15 to 30 cents per mile over the limit, which can add $1,000 or more by the time you turn in the keys.

That's the detail dealership finance managers skip. Buying removes the mileage ceiling entirely, and vehicles over 6,000 pounds can qualify for a Section 179 deduction covering a large chunk of the purchase price in year one. Leasing only lets you deduct the business-use percentage of the monthly payment.

Which option wins depends on your mileage, entity structure, and profit year, not on which payment looks smaller on a dealership worksheet. Our new post breaks down the framework real estate brokers, contractors, and consultants across Tulare and Kings County use to decide.
