## LinkedIn

$9,000 a month. That's the going rate for memory care in Central California, and Medicare pays none of it.

Long-term care lasts about three years on average. Multiply a mid-range monthly cost by 36 months and you're looking at a $300,000 exposure per person. Most families don't plan for this until a parent falls or a diagnosis lands, and by then the options are limited and expensive.

For business owners and professionals across Visalia, Tulare County, Kings County, Fresno County, and Kern County, this decision belongs inside your broader tax and estate strategy, not a solo Google search. Long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance benefits, and self-funding each carry different trade-offs depending on your age, health, and assets. A portion of premiums can even qualify as a medical or business deduction depending on your entity structure.
