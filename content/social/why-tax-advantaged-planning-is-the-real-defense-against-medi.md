## LinkedIn

A household with $150,000 in AGI needs $11,250 in medical costs before the first dollar becomes deductible.

That's the number most business owners never plan around. HSAs, itemized medical deductions, and FSA elections only work if someone maps them against your compensation structure before a health crisis, not after. For 2024, HSA contribution limits sit at $4,150 for individuals and $8,300 for families, with tax-free growth and tax-free qualified withdrawals, something no 401(k) or IRA offers in combination.

This matters most for consultants, healthcare and legal professionals, and technology founders who set their own coverage and pay premiums directly. Bunching elective procedures into one calendar year, structuring HSA contributions through payroll, and timing FSA elections before open enrollment all change the outcome.
