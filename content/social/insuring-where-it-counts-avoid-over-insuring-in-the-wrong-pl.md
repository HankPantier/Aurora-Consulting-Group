## LinkedIn

A $2 million net worth and a $300,000 liability limit. That's the gap we see most often among consultants and business owners building wealth while their insurance hasn't kept pace.

A personal umbrella policy that adds $1-2 million in coverage on top of existing auto and homeowner's policies typically runs $200 to $400 a year. It's one of the cheapest risk transfers available, and it's usually the first thing skipped.

The same math applies to your business: professional liability limits, cyber coverage, and D&O policies should be sized against your actual revenue, payroll, and largest realistic claim, not a generic checklist. For multi-state and multi-entity businesses, that review needs to happen alongside your entity and nexus planning, not months apart.
