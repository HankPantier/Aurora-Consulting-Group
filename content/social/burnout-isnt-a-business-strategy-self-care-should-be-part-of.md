## LinkedIn

Three quarters of late estimated tax payments in a row rarely means someone forgot the deadline. It usually means they're avoiding the numbers because there's nothing left in the tank.

Our team sees the pattern constantly: a SaaS founder who hasn't taken a weekend off in four months, a nonprofit director covering grant reporting and HR alone, a consultant billing 60 hours a week while doing her own books at midnight. The invoice goes out late. The hire gets rushed. The penalty shows up before anyone names the real cause.

Building recurring advisory meetings and delegation into the business protects the owner and the balance sheet. That's the reasoning behind our Team of 3 model: a Client Service Manager, Client Controller, and Client CFO working alongside you for less than one full-time hire, no annual contract required.
