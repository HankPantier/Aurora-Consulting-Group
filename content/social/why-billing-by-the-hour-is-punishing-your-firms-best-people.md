## LinkedIn

A Visalia investor closed on a 14-unit property in Tulare County last spring. Title turned up a lien nobody caught in due diligence, and the attorney handling the cleanup billed by the hour. A two-week fix stretched into five weeks. The final invoice landed 40 percent over the original estimate, and the work wasn't harder, just slower.

That's the flaw baked into hourly billing: it pays professionals more for taking longer, not for solving your problem well. Real estate investors in Tulare, Kings, Fresno, and Kern County feel this on entity formation, cost segregation studies, and entitlement work every time an hourly clock is running.

Value-based pricing flips that. A flat fee for LLC formation, a fixed cost segregation quote, and a milestone fee for entitlement hearings all give you cost certainty before you sign. Aurora prices its own advisory work the same way: fixed monthly, no annual contract.
